## Preserve decisions and boundaries

- D-028: finalize events once, never reopen; later flagged corrections affect reports only. Inventory corrections are separate manual adjustments, with optional explanations.
- D-031: location searches save criteria, not result/quantity snapshots. No stale-count reconciliation or intervening-movement warnings.
