## Constraints to preserve

Orders contribute to Ordered, not relocation In Transit. Only confirmed receipt changes held stock and automatically recalculates catalog cost. Use current held quantity and current stored cost, not purchase history. Unknown current cost (zero/n/a), or nonpositive held quantity, uses the new receipt alone. Nonzero catalog cost displays three decimals outside purchasing. Received purchases cannot reopen; add notes and make separate authorized adjustments. Cancellation preserves order details and clears pending expectation only. Partial deliveries remain deferred FI-001.

Catalog history preserves original entries: initial expectation `[8,000]`, edits `[- 500]` / `[+ 2,000]`, cancellation negative remaining expectation; receipt is a separate actual-stock addition. Purchase notes are immutable, newest first, no pagination, available to every authenticated user in every purchase state.

Retain simple inventory counts without worksheet snapshots or intervening-movement warnings (D-031). No reservations; requests may exceed available stock. Finalized event corrections update reports only, never automatically adjust inventory (D-028).
