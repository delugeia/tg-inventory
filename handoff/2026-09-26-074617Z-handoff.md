## Item work: preserve existing decisions

- Item balances already distinguish storage Available, each location, active events, In Transit, and Ordered (D-033/D-039).
- History already has Date, Description, Quantity, Unit Cost, newest first, date/description sorting, and whole-row links to source transactions. Preserve historical descriptions/costs. Quantity/cost changes belong here; descriptive item edits do not under D-069. History pagination is unspecified; do not copy index no-pagination into it without discussion.
- Purchase history uses bracketed expectations, signed edit deltas, negative remaining cancellation, and separate receipt additions (D-069–D-072). Ordinary relocations have Shipped and Received rows. Do not reopen settled notation.
- Corrections are separate manual location adjustments; explanations optional. Starting inventory uses the note “starting inventory.” Counts use individual units, blank means no change, zero is a count, and saved searches store criteria only. No stale-count warnings, worksheet quantity snapshots, or movement-conflict workflows (D-027–D-032/D-039).
- Current catalog cost is authoritative. Ordinary quantity corrections preserve it; only receipt automatically recalculates it, and admins have a separate override (D-058/D-059). Zero cost displays n/a; nonzero catalog unit cost uses three decimals. Cost override history uses Adjustment with — quantity.
- Finalized event corrections are report-only; no reopening or automatic inventory correction posting (D-028). No reservations; negative recorded balances are allowed (D-035).
